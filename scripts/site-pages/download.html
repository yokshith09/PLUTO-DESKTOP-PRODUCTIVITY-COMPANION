Download Pluto

Version 0.6.0. Free, with no account to make.

WindowsInstaller (.exe or .msi) macOSApple silicon Macs (.dmg)

On Windows

  1. Download the .exe installer and open it.
  2. Windows SmartScreen may say it doesn't recognise the app, because Pluto isn't code-signed yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Pluto opens, and the bear appears at the edge of your screen.

On a Mac

  1. Download the .dmg, open it and drag Pluto into Applications.
  2. Because Pluto isn't signed yet, macOS will stop it the first time. Right-click Pluto in Applications and choose Open, then Open again.
  3. Some extras need a macOS permission, and Pluto asks only when you switch them on: type-along asks for Input Monitoring, and website time asks to read your browser.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Everything in version 0.6.0 is free, with no account. A paid plan with Pluto's own AI is planned for later; see pricing.

Will the pet get in the way?

It hides by itself when a game, film or presentation fills the screen. You can also change its size, make it see-through, or turn it off and keep using the app.

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything works offline. With a connection, Pluto sends its anonymous daily count; without one it just waits.

Does it work on Intel Macs or Linux?

Not yet. The Mac download is for Apple silicon only, and there is no Linux build.

How do I remove it?

Uninstall it like any other app. Your data stays in Pluto's folder until you delete that too; the privacy page says where it is.
