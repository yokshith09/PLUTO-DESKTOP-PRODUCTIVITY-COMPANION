Everything Pluto does

One small app for the whole day: plan it, focus, see where the time went, write things down, and keep a friend at the edge of your screen.

Each part, up close

They work together, so the pet always knows what you're up to.

Today, in one view

The task you're on, what comes next, and a focus timer that the pet keeps beside you. Missed things wait for you here instead of disappearing.

The diary

A private place to end the day. Write a few lines, look back on any day, and let Pluto pick out the things you said you'd do.

  • Locked with your passwordOnly you can open it. Lock now closes it in one click when you step away.
  • A recovery key, shown onceKeep it, and you can set a new password without losing a single entry.
  • Look backMonth tiles show the days you wrote, with totals for the year. Open any day from there.
  • Search every entryFind a word or a name across the whole diary.
  • Spot things to doUnderlines lines like "I need to submit the essay by Friday" and turns them into a task with that due date. Off until you switch it on, and it all happens on your computer.
  • Make a task from any lineSelect some words and make them a task, without leaving the page.
  • Never sharedAssistants can never see the diary, and it is never uploaded.
  • On your computerSaved with the rest of your day in Pluto's own folder. Where exactly.

The little things

Small touches that make it feel like a companion, not another app.

  • Steps aside in full screenThe pet hides by itself while a game, film or presentation fills the screen, and comes back after.
  • Tab TantrumToo many browser tabs open? The pet complains until you're back under your limit, and you can close tabs from Pluto.
  • Daily routineA gentle habit tracker with a ring for today and dots for the week. Nothing turns red.
  • MeetingsNotes, decisions and action items for each meeting, with a follow-up date.
  • LinksThe sites you open every day, one click away on the Today page.
  • Archive and BinNothing you delete is gone straight away. Bring it back if you change your mind.
  • For studentsCourses, a timetable, exam countdowns and class reminders, in a Study page of their own.
  • Five coloursThe app wears the colour you pick, just like this website. Try the dots at the top.
