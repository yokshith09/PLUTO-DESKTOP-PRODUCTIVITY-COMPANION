Meet the companions

Five friends, each drawn by hand, each with its own way of cheering you on. Pick one in the app and it lives at the edge of your screen.

    What every companion does

    The character changes; the habits stay the same.

    • Reacts to your dayFinish, add or start a task and it answers with a jump, a wave or a cheer.
    • Has a specialEvery few finished tasks (or clicks) it does its own trick. Pluto naps, Mochi gets the zoomies.
    • Shows remindersReminders and notes appear in a bubble above the pet, not in a corner of the screen.
    • Gets out of the wayChange its size or how see-through it is, hide it in full screen, or turn it off and keep the app.

    More characters are on the way

    New friends will join in later versions.
