Assistants

Your coding assistant, meet your pet

Pluto works with the AI assistants you already use. They can read your day and add tasks, and the pet tells you when a chat finishes or needs you, so you can step away from the terminal.

Works with

Switch Assistants on in Pluto, then connect each app. Only assistants on this computer can connect.

  • Claude CodeConnect in one click. Tools and pet reactions.
  • CodexConnect in one click. Tools and pet reactions.
  • CursorConnect in one click. Tools and pet reactions.
  • OpenCodeConnect in one click. Tools and pet reactions.
  • VS CodeThe tools, plus pet reactions with a command you copy.
  • WindsurfThe tools, plus pet reactions with a command you copy.
  • AntigravityThe tools, plus pet reactions with a command you copy.
  • Any app that speaks MCPPluto is an MCP server, so other assistants can use its tools too.

What an assistant can do

20 tools in all. You can switch any of them off, one assistant at a time.

Read your day

  • See today's plan and your tasks
  • Find and read your notes
  • Read your reminders and meetings
  • See if you're in a focus session, so it doesn't disturb you

Change things, when you ask

  • Add tasks, reminders and notes
  • Change a task or mark it done
  • Move a reminder or meeting to the Bin
  • Change a setting, only after you press Allow on the pet

Talk through the pet

  • Cheer when a chat finishes, look worried when it's stuck
  • Show a short message, or ask you a quick question with buttons
  • Show how many chats are waiting for you
  • Click the bubble to jump to that chat's window

You stay in charge

  • Off until you switch it on. Nothing can connect before that.
  • Every change can be undone. Anything an assistant adds or changes is listed with an Undo.
  • Quiet while you focus, if you like. Switch it on and assistant messages wait until your focus session ends.
  • Some things stay yours. Assistants can't turn Assistants off, or change tracking, shortcuts or start-up.
  • Never the diary. Assistants can never see it.
  • On your computer. Pluto talks to the assistant on this machine only. Your assistant itself works the way it always does.

Pluto's own AI Coming soon

Today Pluto works with assistants you already have; it has no AI of its own. A paid plan with Pluto's own AI is planned: talk to your pet, help with writing, meeting summaries and quizzes from your notes. Not in 0.6.0.
